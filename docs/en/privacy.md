# Pianyu Privacy Policy

---

- Last updated: November 23, 2026
- Effective date: November 23, 2026

## Introduction

Pianyu ("the App") understands how important your personal information is. We have drawn up this policy in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China and other applicable laws and regulations, to explain how we collect, use, store and protect your information. Please read it carefully before using the App.

## 1. Information We Collect and Use

1. **Library content**: to organize your photos and videos, the App reads thumbnails, names, sizes and timestamps of your photo library after you grant access. All of this is processed on your device and is never uploaded to any server.
2. **Favorites and settings**: the items you favorite, your theme style, group size and cleanup statistics are stored only on your device.
3. The App has no server of its own and integrates no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The web versions of this policy and the user agreement are static pages hosted on GitHub Pages and involve none of your library content.

## 2. System Permissions

1. **Photos and videos**: used to read your library and organize it into groups. Deleting items requires the "read and modify" permission in system settings.
2. **Vibration**: used for haptic feedback when cards land, when you favorite or delete an item; it can be turned off in system settings.
3. **Network**: used only to open the web versions of this policy and the user agreement inside the App. Opening these pages loads term text only; your photos, videos and personal information are never uploaded.

If you deny photo access, the App cannot read your library, but your other local settings are unaffected.

## 3. Storage and Protection

1. **Location**: your settings, favorites and statistics are stored locally on your device by default and are not transferred outside the country.
2. **Retention**: the data is kept while you use the App; uninstalling the App deletes the local data.
3. **Security**: we rely on the app sandbox provided by the system to protect your data. Please understand that no technical measure can guarantee absolute security, so keep your device safe.

## 4. Sharing, Transfer and Disclosure

1. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with any third party.
2. When you actively use the system share feature, the content is handled by the app or system service you choose; please review its privacy policy.
3. We do not publicly disclose your information except as required by laws and regulations or by judicial authorities in accordance with the law.

## 5. Your Rights

1. You can remove favorites, delete content or change group settings in the App at any time.
2. You can restore accidentally deleted items from the system "Recently Deleted".
3. You can uninstall the App to delete all local data.
4. If you have questions or complaints about how your personal information is handled, please contact us using the details below.

## 6. Protection of Minors

The App is mainly intended for adults. If you are under 14, please read this policy with your guardian and use the App only with your guardian's consent.

## 7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates are announced prominently in the App, and material changes require your consent again. We will not reduce your rights under this policy without your explicit consent.
